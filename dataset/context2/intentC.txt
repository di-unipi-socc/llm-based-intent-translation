The cybersecurity provider secureMailOp requires a service to manage 10,000 corporate mailboxes. This pipeline intercepts incoming emails, 
removes phishing links, analyzes attachments in an isolated environment, and delivers only safe messages to the server. Requiring decoding 
to be applied after the initial node, the chain begins with a mailGatewayVF allocated at the edge. Preferring a latency below 50 milliseconds, 
the mailGatewayVF connects to a phishingFilterVF located in the cloud. With a mandatory requirement for bandwidth higher than 100 Mbps, the filter 
links to a sandboxScannerVF located in the cloud. Applying strictly required logging before this node, the sandboxScannerVF is integrated into 
the chain. Finally, the service concludes by connecting the sandboxScannerVF to a safeDeliveryVF node located in the cloud.
