The cybersecurity provider secureMailOp requires a service to manage 10,000 corporate mailboxes. This pipeline intercepts incoming emails, 
removes phishing links, analyzes attachments in an isolated environment, and delivers only safe messages to the server. The service chain includes, 
in order: a mailGatewayVF allocated at the edge, a phishingFilterVF located in the cloud, a sandboxScannerVF located in the cloud, and a safeDeliveryVF 
node located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, decoding must be applied after the mailGatewayVF. Furthermore, the provider prefers to keep the 
latency from the mailGatewayVF to the phishingFilterVF below 50 milliseconds. It is strictly required that the bandwidth from the phishingFilterVF to 
the sandboxScannerVF is higher than 100 Mbps. Finally, logging is mandatory before the sandboxScannerVF.
