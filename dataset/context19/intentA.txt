The logistics provider aeroDeliveryOp requires a drone fleet control service to manage 5,000 active delivery drones. This system manages digital 
flight plans, avoids software collisions, and ensures that the command signal is always a priority. The deployed service chain includes, in order: 
a flightTelemetryIngesterVF allocated at the edge, a collisionAvoiderVF located in the cloud, a commandPrioritizerVF located in the cloud, and a routeDispatcherVF allocated at the edge. Regarding requirements, caching is applied after the flightTelemetryIngesterVF. Furthermore, the provider prefers 
that the total chain hardware remains lower than 200 GB. It is strictly required that the latency from the flightTelemetryIngesterVF to the collisionAvoiderVF remains below 50 ms. Finally, the provider prefers that the bandwidth from the collisionAvoiderVF to the routeDispatcherVF remains higher than 2000 Mbps.
