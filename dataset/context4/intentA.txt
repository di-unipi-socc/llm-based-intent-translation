The compliance provider gdprComplianceOp requires a GDPR verification service to manage 5,000 corporate database connections. This service scans 
corporate databases to identify unprotected personal data, applies automatic anonymization, and generates reports for regulators. The deployed service 
chain includes, in order: a dbScannerVF allocated at the edge, an anonymizerVF in the cloud, and a reportGeneratorVF located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, privacy must be applied after the dbScannerVF. Furthermore, it is desired that the latency from the dbScannerVF to the 
anonymizerVF remains lower than 50 ms. It is strictly required that the bandwidth from the anonymizerVF to the reportGeneratorVF is higher than 50 Mbps. Finally, 
security is mandatory before the reportGeneratorVF.
