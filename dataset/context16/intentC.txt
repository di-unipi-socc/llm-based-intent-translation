The service provider eInvoiceOp requires a digital invoicing service to manage 50,000 invoices per day. This system transforms sales data into certified XML files, 
applies the digital signature, and automatically sends them to governmental systems for fiscal validation. Mandating that the total chain hardware remains lower 
than 150 GB, the service begins with a saleDataCollectorVF allocated at the edge connected to an xmlConverterVF located in the cloud. Strictly requiring that the 
latency from the xmlConverterVF to the digitalSignerVF remains below 30 ms, the chain incorporates a digitalSignerVF located in the cloud. Finally, preferring 
that the bandwidth from the digitalSignerVF to the final point remains higher than 100 Mbps, the system completes the pipeline with a taxAuthorityTransmitterVF located in the cloud.
