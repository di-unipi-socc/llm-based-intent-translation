The service provider eInvoiceOp requires a digital invoicing service to manage 50,000 invoices per day. This system transforms sales data into certified XML files, 
applies the digital signature, and automatically sends them to governmental systems for fiscal validation. The service chain begins with a saleDataCollectorVF 
allocated at the edge. For the entire deployment, it is mandatory that the total chain hardware remains lower than 150 GB. The chain then connects to an xmlConverterVF located in the cloud. It is strictly required that the latency from this xmlConverterVF to the next component, a digitalSignerVF located in the cloud, 
remains below 30 ms. Finally, the files are routed to ataxAuthorityTransmitterVF located in the cloud, and it is ideally preferred that the bandwidth from the 
digitalSignerVF to this taxAuthorityTransmitterVF remains higher than 100 Mbps.
