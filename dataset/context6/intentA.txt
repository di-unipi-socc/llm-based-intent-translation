The government provider govMessagingOp requires a service to manage 50,000 institutional messages. This service receives digital communications,
verifies the electronic signature of the sender, checks its integrity, and archives the message in a legal vault. The deployed service chain 
includes, in order: a msgReceiverVF allocated at the edge, a sigVerifierVF located in the cloud, an integrityCheckerVF located in the 
cloud, and a legalVaultVF located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, caching is applied after the msgReceiverVF. Furthermore, the provider 
prefers to keep the total chain hardware lower than 100 GB. It is a mandatory requirement that the sigVerifierVF and the integrityCheckerVF 
operate on the same node. Finally, compression is applied before the legalVaultVF.
