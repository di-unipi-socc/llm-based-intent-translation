The government provider govMessagingOp requires a service to manage 50,000 institutional messages. This service receives digital communications, 
verifies the electronic signature of the sender, checks its integrity, and archives the message in a legal vault. Specifying that caching is 
applied after the first function, the service begins with a msgReceiverVF allocated at the edge. Mandating that the sigVerifierVF and the integrityCheckerVF are deployed on the same hosting node, the chain connects to a sigVerifierVF located in the cloud and 
an integrityCheckerVF located in the cloud. Preferring that the total chain hardware is lower than 100 GB, and ensuring that compression is 
executed before the final legalVaultVF, the system completes the pipeline with a legalVaultVF located in the cloud.
