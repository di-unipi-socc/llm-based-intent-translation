The educational provider eduCloudOp requires a virtual student lab platform to support 10,000 concurrent students. This system provides temporary programming 
environments, manages resource allocation for each student, and monitors the workspaces to prevent improper use. The service chain begins with a sessionIngesterVF allocated at the edge, data privacy mechanisms are applied after it. The system then connects to a resourceAllocatorVF located in the cloud, ensuring that the network capacity between the sessionIngesterVF and this resourceAllocatorVF supports more than 500 Mbps. 
The pipeline continues to an activityMonitorVF located in the cloud, ensuring that security are executed before it, and that the latency time 
from the sessionIngesterVF to this activityMonitorVF is kept under 55 ms. The next blocks are a userPrivacyEnforcerVF located in the cloud and an auditLoggerVF 
located in the cloud, where logging are executed before the auditLoggerVF. Finally, the system routes the results to an outputPackagerVF located in the cloud, 
specifying that compression is applied after it. For the whole deployment, the provider prefers that the aggregate infrastructure footprint stays below 120 GB.
