The educational provider eduCloudOp requires a virtual student lab platform to support 10,000 concurrent students. This system provides 
temporary programming environments, manages resource allocation for each student, and monitors the workspaces to prevent improper use. The deployed service 
chain includes, in order: a sessionIngesterVF allocated at the edge, a resourceAllocatorVF located in the cloud, an activityMonitorVF located in the cloud, a userPrivacyEnforcerVF located in the cloud, an auditLoggerVF located in the cloud, and an outputPackagerVF located in the cloud. Regarding constraints, data privacy 
mechanisms are applied after the sessionIngesterVF. To detect anomalies early, security are executed before the activityMonitorVF, while ensuring that 
the latency time between the initial sessionIngesterVF and this activityMonitorVF is kept under 55 ms. Furthermore, logging operations are executed 
before the auditLoggerVF. Data compression is applied after the final outputPackagerVF. For the network backbone, the provider requires that the bandwidth between 
the sessionIngesterVF and the resourceAllocatorVF supports more than 500 Mbps. Finally, the provider prefers that the aggregate infrastructure footprint stays below 120 GB.
