The IT provider corpITOp requires a service to manage 5,000 office employees. This service must filter dangerous websites, 
scan downloaded files for malware, and ensure that sensitive data does not leave the network. The service chain begins by connecting a webFilterVF 
allocated at the edge to a malwareScannerVF located in the cloud; for this specific segment, it is mandatory that the latency remains below 25 milliseconds.
Subsequently, the chain proceeds by connecting the malwareScannerVF to a dlpEngineVF located in the cloud, ensuring a bandwidth greater than 500 Mbps 
on this final link.
