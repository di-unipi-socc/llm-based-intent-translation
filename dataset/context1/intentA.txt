The IT provider corpITOp requires a service to manage 5,000 office employees. This service must filter dangerous websites, 
scan downloaded files for malware, and ensure that sensitive data does not leave the network. The service chain includes, 
in order: a webFilterVF allocated at the edge, a malwareScannerVF located in the cloud, and a dlpEngineVF located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, it is a mandatory requirement that the latency from webFilterVF to the malwareScannerVF remains below 25 milliseconds. 
Furthermore, the bandwidth between the malwareScannerVF and the dlpEngineVF must be greater than 500 Mbps.
