The healthcare provider healthCareOp requires a post-operative tele-monitoring service to manage 5,000 active patients. 
This service receives digitalized patient vital data from home, analyzes cardiac anomalies, and notifies the doctor on duty immediately in case of danger. 
Specifying that privacy is applied after the initial unit, the service begins with a patientDataReceiverVF allocated at the edge connected to a 
cardiacAnomalyAnalyzerVF located in the cloud. Ensuring that logging is executed before, the chain incorporates a medicalAlertLoggerVF 
located in the cloud. Finally, strictly requiring that the latency from the patientDataReceiverVF to the final node remains below 100 ms, 
and preferring that the total chain hardware remains lower than 80 GB, the system completes the pipeline with a doctorNotifierVF located in the cloud.
