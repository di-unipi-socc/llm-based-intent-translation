The healthcare provider healthCareOp requires a post-operative tele-monitoring service to manage 5,000 active patients. This service receives digitalized patient vital 
data from home, analyzes cardiac anomalies, and notifies the doctor on duty immediately in case of danger. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a patientDataReceiverVF allocated at the edge, a cardiacAnomalyAnalyzerVF located in the cloud, a medicalAlertLoggerVF located in the cloud, and a doctorNotifierVF located 
in the cloud. Regarding requirements, privacy is applied after the patientDataReceiverVF. Furthermore, logging is executed before the medicalAlertLoggerVF. 
It is strictly required that the latency from the patientDataReceiverVF to the doctorNotifierVF remains below 100 ms. Finally, the provider prefers that the 
total chain hardware remains lower than 80 GB.
