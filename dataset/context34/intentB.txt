The securityOp provider intends to deploy the service to manage 50 users for factory perimeter monitoring and night intrusion detection. The chain begins with cameraIngestVF allocated at the edge and intrusionDetectVF allocated at the edge. Data caching is strictly required after cameraIngestVF, and we prefer to maintain a bandwidth greater than 50 megabps between cameraIngestVF and intrusionDetectVF. Additionally, a dedicated node is mandatory for intrusionDetectVF. Next, the flow continues to alarmDispatcherVF located in the cloud and finishes at videoStorageVF located in the cloud, where privacy must be inserted before videoStorageVF.
