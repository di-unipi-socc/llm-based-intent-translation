The securityOp provider intends to deploy the service to manage 50 users for factory perimeter monitoring and night intrusion detection. The network service chain consists of cameraIngestVF located at the edge, intrusionDetectVF located at the edge, alarmDispatcherVF located in the cloud, and videoStorageVF located in the cloud. It is mandatory that data caching is applied after cameraIngestVF, and privacy is strictly required before videoStorageVF. Regarding performance, a dedicated node is strictly required for intrusionDetectVF, while we prefer to guarantee a bandwidth greater than 50 megabps from cameraIngestVF to intrusionDetectVF.
