The media provider mediaBroadcastOp requires a live multicamera streaming service to support 30,000 concurrent viewers. 
This system receives video streams from different angles, allows cloud mixing, adds advertising graphics, and distributes the signal to 
thousands of viewers simultaneously. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a streamIngesterVF allocated at the edge, a cloudStudioVF 
located in the cloud, a graphicInjectorVF located in the cloud, and a streamDistributorVF located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, 
compression is applied after the streamIngesterVF. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the bandwidth from the graphicInjectorVF to 
the streamDistributorVF remains higher than 5000 Mbps. Additionally, the provider prefers that the total chain hardware is lower than 150 GB. 
Finally, caching is executed before the streamDistributorVF.
