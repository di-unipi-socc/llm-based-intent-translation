The enterprise provider corpDocOp requires a document classification engine to manage 50,000 daily files. This system analyzes PDF files or 
OCR scans, identifies the document type like invoice, contract, or reminder, and routes it to the competent business function. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: an ocrScannerVF allocated at the edge, a textExtractorVF located in the edge, an aiDocumentClassifierVF located in the cloud, a dataRedactorVF located in the cloud, and adocumentRouterVF located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, 
security is applied after the ocrScannerVF. Furthermore, it is a mandatory requirement that the ocrScannerVF and the textExtractorVF are deployed 
on the same hosting node. It is strictly required that the latency from the textExtractorVF to the aiDocumentClassifierVF remains below 50 ms. 
Additionally, privacy is executed before the dataRedactorVF. Finally, the provider prefers that the bandwidth from the aiDocumentClassifierVF to 
the documentRouterVF remains higher than 500 Mbps.
