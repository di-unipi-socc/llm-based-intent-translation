The enterprise provider corpDocOp requires a document classification engine to manage 50,000 daily files. This system analyzes PDF 
files or OCR scans, identifies the document type like invoice, contract, or reminder, and routes it to the competent business function. 
Specifying that security is applied after the initial phase, and mandating that this first component and the textExtractorVF are deployed on the same hosting node, the service begins with an ocrScannerVF allocated at the edge connected to a textExtractorVF 
located in the edge. Strictly requiring that the latency from the textExtractorVF to the aiDocumentClassifierVF remains below 50 ms, the 
chain incorporates an aiDocumentClassifierVF located in the cloud. Ensuring that privacy is executed before the dataRedactorVF, the chain connects 
to a dataRedactorVF located in the cloud. Finally, preferring a bandwidth higher than 500 Mbps between the aiDocumentClassifierVF and the final 
distribution point, the system completes the pipeline with a documentRouterVF located in the cloud.
