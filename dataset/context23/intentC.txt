The healthcare provider medDiagnosticOp requires a radiology AI diagnostics service to manage 10,000 daily scans. 
This pipeline receives high-resolution medical images, processes them with computer vision algorithms to highlight suspicious lesions, and stores them securely. 
Specifying that data compression is applied after the initial receiving function, the service begins with a dicomIngesterVF allocated at the edge. 
Ensuring that the network bandwidth from this first component to thelesionAnalyzerVF remains higher than 500 Mbps, the chain connects to a lesionAnalyzerVF located in the cloud. Mandating that thislesionAnalyzerVF and the subsequent metadataExtractorVF are strictly deployed on dedicated infrastructure servers, 
the chain incorporates both blocks at the cloud. Finally, ensuring encoding is executed before the final unit, the system completes the pipeline 
with a secureArchiverVF located in the cloud.
