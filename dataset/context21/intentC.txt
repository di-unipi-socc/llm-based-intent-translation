The industrial provider autoWarehouseOp requires a remote control system for autonomous machines to manage 500 warehouse robots. 
This software receives sensor data, calculates paths to avoid collisions, and sends steering and braking commands. Specifying that decoding 
is applied after the initial node, the service begins with a sensorDataDecoderVF allocated at the edge connected to a collisionPathSolverVF located in the cloud. Strictly requiring that the latency from the sensorDataDecoderVF to commandEncoderVF remains below 500 ms, and ensuring that encoding is executed 
before the final instruction generator, the system completes the pipeline with a commandEncoderVF allocated at the edge.
