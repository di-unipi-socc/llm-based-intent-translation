The industrial provider autoWarehouseOp requires a remote control system for autonomous machines to manage 500 warehouse robots. 
This software receives sensor data, calculates paths to avoid collisions, and sends steering and braking commands. The deployed service chain includes, 
in order: a sensorDataDecoderVF allocated at the edge, a collisionPathSolverVF located in the cloud, and a commandEncoderVF allocated at the edge. 
Regarding requirements, decoding is applied after the sensorDataDecoderVF. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the latency from the sensorDataDecoderVF 
to the commandEncoderVF remains below 500 ms. Finally, encoding is executed before the commandEncoderVF.
