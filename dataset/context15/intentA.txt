The financial provider onlineBankOp requires a digital identity verification service to manage 10,000 daily uploads. This service processes photos of 
documents uploaded by users, verifies their authenticity, and compares the data with international blacklists to prevent money laundering. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: a documentReceiverVF allocated at the edge, an authenticityCheckerVF located in the cloud, an amlMatcherVF 
located in the cloud, and a profileCreatorVF located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, encoding is applied after the documentReceiverVF. Security is 
executed before the authenticityCheckerVF. Privacy is applied after the amlMatcherVF. Finally, compression is executed before the profileCreatorVF.
