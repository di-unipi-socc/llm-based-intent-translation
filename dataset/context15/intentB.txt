The financial provider onlineBankOp requires a digital identity verification service to manage 10,000 daily uploads. 
This service processes photos of documents uploaded by users, verifies their authenticity, and compares the data with international blacklists to prevent 
money laundering. The service chain begins with a documentReceiverVF allocated at the edge. For this component, encoding is applied after it. The chain then 
connects to an authenticityCheckerVF located in the cloud, ensuring that security is executed before it. The chain proceeds to an amlMatcherVF located in the cloud; 
for this component, privacy is applied after it. Finally, the processed data is routed to a profileCreatorVF located in the cloud, ensuring that compression 
is executed before it.
