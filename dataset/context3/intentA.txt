The website security provider ddosProtectionOp requires an anti-DDoS defense system to handle 50,000 active web users. This system analyzes incoming traffic, 
cleans malicious flows, and ensures server availability. The deployed service chain consists of: a networkTrafficAnalyzerVF located at the edge, a scrubbingFilterVF 
located in the cloud, a rateLimiterVF located in the cloud, and a webServerVF located in the cloud. For security operations, it is mandatory that security is 
applied before the scrubbingFilterVF. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the bandwidth between the networkTrafficAnalyzerVF and the scrubbingFilterVF is greater 
than 500 megabps. Additionally, the provider prefers to enforce logging after the rateLimiterVF. Finally, it is desired that the latency from the rateLimiterVF 
to the webServerVF remains lower than 30 ms.
