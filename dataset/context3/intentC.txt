The website security provider ddosProtectionOp requires an anti-DDoS defense system to handle 50,000 active web users. This system analyzes incoming traffic,
cleans malicious flows, and ensures server availability. Demanding a strictly required bandwidth greater than 500 megabps between them, the service begins
with a networkTrafficAnalyzerVF located at the edge linked to a scrubbingFilterVF located in the cloud. Mandating that security is processed before the scrubbingFilterVF, 
the chain then proceeds to a rateLimiterVF located in the cloud. Finally, specifying that logging should ideally be performed after the rateLimiterVF, and preferring 
a latency lower than 30 ms, the system connects to the final webServerVF located in the cloud.
