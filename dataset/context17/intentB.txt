The logistics provider deliveryLogisticsOp requires a route optimization service to manage 15,000 couriers. This software receives the GPS positions of a fleet, 
analyzes city traffic data, constantly recalculates the best route for each courier, and updates their terminals. The service chain begins with a gpsIngesterVF 
allocated at the edge. For this component, compression is applied after it. The chain then connects to a trafficAnalyzerVF located in the cloud followed by a 
routeOptimizerVF located in the cloud. The updated routes are routed to a terminalUpdaterVF allocated at the edge, ensuring that decoding is executed before it. 
For the entire deployment, it is strictly required that the latency from the begin to the end of the chain must remain below 80 ms.
