The logistics provider deliveryLogisticsOp requires a route optimization service to manage 15,000 couriers. This software receives the GPS positions of a fleet, 
analyzes city traffic data, constantly recalculates the best route for each courier, and updates their terminals. Specifying that compression is applied after the 
initial node, the service begins with a gpsIngesterVF allocated at the edge connected to a trafficAnalyzerVF located in the cloud and a routeOptimizerVF 
located in the cloud. Ensuring that decoding is executed before the final node, the system incorporates a terminalUpdaterVF allocated at the edge. 
Finally, strictly requiring that the latency from the begin to the end of the chain remains below 80 ms, the system completes the optimization pipeline.
