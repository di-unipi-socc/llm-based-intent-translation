The media provider vodStreamOp requires a video on demand distribution service to manage 80,000 active subscribers. 
This service optimizes movie delivery by encrypting content with DRM, compressing it into different formats, and storing it on 
nodes close to the user to avoid buffering. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a drmEncryptorVF allocated in the cloud, 
a transcoderVF allocated in the cloud, and a cachingNodeVF node allocated at the edge. Regarding requirements, encoding is applied after 
the drmEncryptorVF. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the bandwidth from the transcoderVF to the cachingNodeVF 
node remains higher than 10000 Mbps. Finally, the provider prefers that the drmEncryptorVF and the transcoderVF operate on the same node, 
while decoding is applied before the cachingNodeVF node.
