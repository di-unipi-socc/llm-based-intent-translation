Media provider vodStreamOp needs a video-on-demand distribution service to handle 80,000 active subscribers. 
This service optimizes movie distribution by encrypting content with DRM, compressing it into various formats, and storing it on nodes close 
to the user to avoid buffering. By specifying that encoding be applied after the initial drmEncryptorVF and preferring that this first 
component and the subsequent transcoderVF service be deployed on the same hosting node, the service begins with a drmEncryptorVF hosted in the 
cloud and connected to a transcoderVF also hosted in the cloud. Requiring bandwidth exceeding 10,000 Mbps before moving on to the next element, 
the chain includes a cachingNodeVF node hosted at the edge. Finally, by ensuring that decoding is performed before the cachingNodeVF at the edge, 
the system completes the video-on-demand distribution pipeline.
