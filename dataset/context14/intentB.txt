The financial provider hftGlobalOp requires a high frequency trading service to manage 500,000 orders. This infrastructure receives stock prices, 
executes decision algorithms, and sends purchase orders seeking the fastest network path. The service chain begins with a marketFeedVF allocated at the edge. 
For this component, caching is applied after it. The chain then connects to a tradingAlgoVF allocated at the edge, ensuring that it is mandatory for the latency 
from the marketFeedVF to this tradingAlgoVF to remain below 50 ms. The chain proceeds to a riskCheckerVF located in the cloud; it is ideally preferred that the 
bandwidth from the tradingAlgoVF to this riskCheckerVF remains higher than 5000 Mbps. Finally, the system routes to an orderDispatcherVF allocated at the edge, 
with a preference that the latency from the riskCheckerVF to this orderDispatcherVF remains below 60 ms, and ensuring that logging is executed before it.
