The financial provider hftGlobalOp requires a high frequency trading service to manage 500,000 orders. This infrastructure receives stock prices, 
executes decision algorithms, and sends purchase orders seeking the fastest network path. Specifying that caching is applied after the initial function, the service begins with a marketFeedVF allocated at the edge. Mandating that the latency from this first component to the tradingAlgoVF remains 
below 50 ms, the chain connects to a tradingAlgoVF allocated at the edge. Preferring that the bandwidth from the tradingAlgoVF to the riskCheckerVF remains 
higher than 5000 Mbps, the chain incorporates a riskCheckerVF located in the cloud. Preferring that the latency from the riskCheckerVF to the final node remains below 60 ms, and ensuring that logging is executed before this final component, the system completes the pipeline with an orderDispatcherVF allocated 
at the edge.
