The infrastructure provider serverMonOp requires a telemetry monitoring service to manage 50,000 virtual machines. This service collects temperature, 
CPU load, and memory usage data, aggregating them to identify anomalies before failure occurs. Specifying that it is mandatory for the telemetryCollectorVF and the dataAggregatorVF to be deployed on the same hosting node, the service begins with a telemetryCollectorVF at the edge connected 
to a dataAggregatorVF at the edge. Strictly requiring that the latency from the begin to the end of the chain remains below 100 ms, and preferring 
that the bandwidth from the begin to the end of the chain remains higher than 2000 Mbps, the chain incorporates an anomalyDetectorVF in the cloud. 
Finally, the system completes the pipeline with an alertDispatcherVF in the cloud.
