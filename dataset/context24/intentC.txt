The digital provider secureVoteOp requires a certified electronic voting service to manage 200,000 voters. This platform for public or corporate consultations 
guarantees vote anonymity and prevents data manipulation. Preferring that the network bandwidth from the entry unit to the next phase remains higher than 
100 Mbps, the service begins with a ballotIngesterVF allocated at the edge connected to an anonymizerVoteVF located in the cloud. Specifying that user privacy is 
applied after this anonymizerVoteVF process, and ensuring that security is executed before the integrityEnforcerVF, the chain incorporates an integrityEnforcerVF 
located in the cloud. The pipeline proceeds to a voteCounterVF located in the cloud. Finally, ensuring that logging is executed before the last
unit, the system completes the pipeline with an electoralAuditLoggerVF located in the cloud.
