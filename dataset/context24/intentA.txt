The digital provider secureVoteOp requires a certified electronic voting service to manage 200,000 voters. This platform for public or corporate consultations 
guarantees vote anonymity and prevents data manipulation. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a ballotIngesterVF allocated at the edge, an anonymizerVoteVF 
located in the cloud, an integrityEnforcerVF located in the cloud, a voteCounterVF located in the cloud, and an electoralAuditLoggerVF located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, the provider prefers that the network bandwidth from the ballotIngesterVF to the anonymizerVoteVF remains higher than 100 Mbps. 
Furthermore, user privacy is applied after the anonymizerVoteVF. Security is executed before the integrityEnforcerVF to protect the system from external manipulation. 
Finally, logging is executed before the electoralAuditLoggerVF to compile compliance history.
