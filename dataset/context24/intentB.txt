The digital provider secureVoteOp requires a certified electronic voting service to manage 200,000 voters. This platform for public or corporate consultations 
guarantees vote anonymity and prevents data manipulation. The service chain begins with a ballotIngesterVF allocated at the edge. The chain then connects 
to an anonymizerVoteVF located in the cloud, prefer that the network bandwidth from the ballotIngesterVF to this anonymizerVoteVF remains higher than 
100 Mbps, while privacy is applied after it. The pipeline continues to an integrityEnforcerVF located in the cloud, ensuring that security is executed before it. 
The system then routes the data to a voteCounterVF located in the cloud. Finally, the processed data flows to an electoralAuditLoggerVF located in the cloud, ensuring 
that logging is executed before it.
