Cloud gaming service provider cloudGamingOp needs a service to handle 40,000 active players. This service runs games on powerful servers and 
delivers the video stream to the user, ensuring very low latency to prevent the game from becoming unplayable. Specifying that encoding 
be applied after the initial gameExecutorVF, the service begins with a gameExecutorVF hosted in the cloud. Preferring that this first component 
operate on a dedicated node, the chain includes a streamPackerVF hosted in the cloud. Finally, strictly requiring that the latency from 
the streamPackerVF to the final deliveryOptimizerVF remain less than 30 ms and it is prefered the streamPackerVF operates on a dedicated node, the system 
completes the pipeline with a deliveryOptimizerVF hosted at the edge.
