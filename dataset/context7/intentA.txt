The gaming provider cloudGamingOp requires a service to manage 40,000 active gamers. This service executes video games on powerful servers 
and sends the video stream to the user, guaranteeing a very low latency to avoid making the game unplayable. The deployed service chain 
includes, in order: a gameExecutorVF allocated in the cloud, a streamPackerVF allocated in the cloud, and a deliveryOptimizerVF allocated 
at the edge. Regarding requirements, encoding is applied after the gameExecutorVF. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the latency 
from the streamPackerVF to the deliveryOptimizerVF remains below 30 ms. Finally, the provider prefers that the gameExecutorVF and the streamPackerVF operate on dedicated nodes.
