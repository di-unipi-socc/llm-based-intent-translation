The network security provider secureCryptoOp requires an encrypted traffic analysis service to support 25,000 corporate users. This service inspects HTTPS flows 
without fully decrypting them, using AI to identify malware or botnet patterns. Specifying that decoding is applied after the first VNF, and mandating that 
this initial component and the subsequent featureExtractorVF service are deployed on the same hosting node, the service begins with a trafficSplitterVF allocated at 
the edge connected to a featureExtractorVF located in the edge. Preferring that the total chain hardware is lower than 80 gigabytes, the chain incorporates 
an aiClassifierVF located in the cloud followed by an alertManagerVF located in the cloud. Finally, ensuring that security is executed before the final policyEnforcerVF point, 
the system completes the pipeline with a policyEnforcerVF located in the cloud.
