The network security provider secureCryptoOp requires an encrypted traffic analysis service to support 25,000 corporate users. This service inspects HTTPS flows 
without fully decrypting them, using AI to identify malware or botnet patterns. The deployed service chain includes, in order: trafficSplitterVF allocated at 
the edge, a featureExtractorVF located in the edge, an aiClassifierVF located in the cloud, an alertManagerVF located in the cloud, and a policyEnforcerVF located 
in the cloud. Regarding requirements, decoding is applied after the trafficSplitterVF. Furthermore, the provider prefers to keep the total chain hardware lower 
than 80 gigabytes. It is mandatory that the trafficSplitterVF and the featureExtractorVF are deployed on the same hosting node. Finally, security is enforced 
before the policyEnforcerVF.
