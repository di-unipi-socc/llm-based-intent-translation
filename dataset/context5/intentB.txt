The network security provider secureCryptoOp requires an encrypted traffic analysis service to support 25,000 corporate users. This service inspects HTTPS flows 
without fully decrypting them, using AI to identify malware or botnet patterns. The service chain begins with a trafficSplitterVF allocated at the edge. For this 
component, decoding is applied after it. It is mandatory that this trafficSplitterVF and the next featureExtractorVF, located in the edge, are deployed on the same 
hosting node. The chain then proceeds to an aiClassifierVF located in the cloud, followed by an alertManagerVF located in the cloud. For the entire deployment, it is 
ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 80 gigabytes. Finally, the traffic is routed to a policyEnforcerVF located in the cloud, ensuring 
that security is executed before the policyEnforcerVF.
