The smartFactoryOp provider intends to deploy the oeeMonitoringService to manage 300 users for real-time machine status tracking and line efficiency analysis. The service processing chain consists of machineIngestVF located at the edge, oeeAggregatorVF located at the edge, alertDispatcherVF located in the cloud, and oeeAnalyticsVF located in the cloud. Regarding service modifications, logging must be applied after machineIngestVF, and security strictly requires to be configured before alertDispatcherVF. For operational requirements, a latency lower than 15 ms is strictly required between machineIngestVF and oeeAggregatorVF, alertDispatcherVF must run on a dedicated host, and a bandwidth higher than 20 megabps is preferred between oeeAggregatorVF and alertDispatcherVF.
