The smartFactoryOp provider intends to deploy the oeeMonitoringService to manage 300 users for real-time machine status tracking and line efficiency analysis. Mandating logging right after machineIngestVF and a strictly required latency lower than 15 ms between machineIngestVF and oeeAggregatorVF, the processing chain begins with machineIngestVF allocated at the edge and oeeAggregatorVF allocated at the edge. Requiring security before alertDispatcherVF, a dedicated host strictly required for alertDispatcherVF, and a preferred bandwidth exceeding 20 megabps between oeeAggregatorVF and alertDispatcherVF, the flow continues to alertDispatcherVF in the cloud and finishes at oeeAnalyticsVF in the cloud.
