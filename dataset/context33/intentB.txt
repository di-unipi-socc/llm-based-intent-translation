The smartFactoryOp provider intends to deploy the oeeMonitoringService to manage 300 users for real-time machine status tracking and line efficiency analysis. The chain begins with machineIngestVF allocated at the edge and oeeAggregatorVF allocated at the edge, mandating logging immediately after machineIngestVF, where a latency lower than 15 ms is strictly required between machineIngestVF and oeeAggregatorVF. Next, the flow continues to alertDispatcherVF located in the cloud, which strictly requires security before it and must be isolated on a dedicated node, with a bandwidth higher than 20 megabps preferred between oeeAggregatorVF and alertDispatcherVF. Finally, the service finishes at oeeAnalyticsVF located in the cloud.
