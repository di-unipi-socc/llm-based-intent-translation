The municipal provider smartParkOp requires a parking management backend to handle 100,000 active parking spots. This system receives occupancy alerts, 
updates the city map, and processes automatic payments. The service chain includes, in order: a sensorDataCollectorVF at the edge, a mapUpdaterVF in the cloud, 
a paymentProcessorVF in the cloud, a statusLoggerVF in the cloud, and a securityEnforcerVF in the cloud. Regarding requirements, compression is applied after the 
sensorDataCollectorVF. Encoding is executed before the paymentProcessorVF. Security is applied after the paymentProcessorVF. Logging is executed before the statusLoggerVF. 
Furthermore, it is mandatory that the mapUpdaterVF and the paymentProcessorVF are deployed on dedicated nodes. Finally, it is strictly 
required that the latency from the sensorDataCollectorVF to the securityEnforcerVF remains below 100 ms.
