The digital provider subscriptionOp requires a SaaS subscription management service to manage 50,000 active accounts. This recurring billing service handles 
customer payment cycles, software plan upgrades, and automated reminders for expired credit cards. The deployed service chain includes, in order: 
a subscriptionIngesterVF allocated at the edge, a billingCycleProcessorVF located in the cloud, a dunningManagerVF located in the cloud, and an invoiceGeneratorVF located in the cloud. Regarding operational requirements, customer privacy is applied after the subscriptionIngesterVF. To interpret the incoming 
API payloads safely, decoding is executed before the billingCycleProcessorVF. Furthermore, transaction logging is executed before the dunningManagerVF to 
keep historical track of payment failures. Finally, encoding is executed before the invoiceGeneratorVF to protect sensitive financial records.
