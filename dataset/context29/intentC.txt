The digital provider subscriptionOp requires a SaaS subscription management service to manage 50,000 active accounts. This recurring billing service handles 
customer payment cycles, software plan upgrades, and automated reminders for expired credit cards. Specifying that customer privacy is applied after the 
initial point, the service begins with a subscriptionIngesterVF allocated at the edge. Ensuring that decoding is executed before the billingCycleProcessorVF, the chain connects to a billingCycleProcessorVF located in the cloud. Mandating that transaction logging is executed before the dunningManagerVF, 
the pipeline incorporates a dunningManagerVF located in the cloud. Finally, ensuring encoding is executed before the invoiceGeneratorVF stage, the system 
completes the pipeline with an invoiceGeneratorVF located in the cloud.
