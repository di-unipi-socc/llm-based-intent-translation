The media provider cinemaRenderOp requires a service to manage 5,000 rendering projects. This infrastructure receives raw digital scenes, 
distributes the rendering computation across hundreds of virtual nodes, and reassembles the finished frames for post-production. Preferring 
that the total chain hardware is lower than 800 GB. Specifying that compression is applied after the initial function, the service 
begins with a sceneReceiverVF allocated at the edge connected to an assetValidatorVF located in the cloud, a renderDistributorVF located in the 
cloud, and a frameAssemblerVF located in the cloud. Ensuring that caching is executed before the final node, the system completes 
the pipeline with a colorGraderVF located in the cloud.
