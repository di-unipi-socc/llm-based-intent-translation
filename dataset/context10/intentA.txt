The media provider cinemaRenderOp requires a service to manage 5,000 rendering projects. This infrastructure receives raw digital scenes, 
distributes the rendering computation across hundreds of virtual nodes, and reassembles the finished frames for post-production. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: a sceneReceiverVF allocated at the edge, an assetValidatorVF located in the cloud, 
a renderDistributorVF located in the cloud, a frameAssemblerVF located in the cloud, and a colorGraderVF located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, compression is applied after the sceneReceiverVF. Furthermore, the provider prefers that the total chain hardware 
remains lower than 800 GB. Finally, caching is executed before the colorGraderVF.
