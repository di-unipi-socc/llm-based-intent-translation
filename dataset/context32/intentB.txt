The steelIndustryOp provider intends to deploy the digitalTwinService to manage 1500 users. The service manages a digital twin of a blast furnace and requires continuous synchronization of physical parameters (pressure, temperature) with the 3D model hosted in the cloud to simulate thermal efficiency in real time. The chain begins with sensorIngestVF allocated at the edge and dataFilteringVF allocated at the edge, mandating data compression immediately after sensorIngestVF. Next, the flow continues to thermalSimVF located in the cloud, where data decoding is strictly necessary before thermalSimVF and a latency lower than 35 ms is mandatory between sensorIngestVF and thermalSimVF. Finally, the service finishes at model3dVF located in the cloud. It is desired to keep total chain hardware consumption below 80 GB.
