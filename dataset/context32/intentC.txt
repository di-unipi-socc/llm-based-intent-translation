The steelIndustryOp provider intends to deploy the digitalTwinService to manage 1500 users. The service manages a digital twin of a blast furnace and requires continuous synchronization of physical parameters (pressure, temperature) with the 3D model hosted in the cloud to simulate thermal efficiency in real time. With data compression strictly required after sensorIngestVF and a mandatory latency lower than 35 ms between sensorIngestVF and thermalSimVF, the processing chain begins with sensorIngestVF allocated at the edge and dataFilteringVF allocated at the edge. Requiring payload decoding before thermalSimVF and ideally keeping total chain hardware consumption below 80 GB, the flow continues to thermalSimVF in the cloud and finishes at model3dVF in the cloud.
