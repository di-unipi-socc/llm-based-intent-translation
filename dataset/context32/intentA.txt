The steelIndustryOp provider intends to deploy the digitalTwinService to manage 1500 users. The service manages a digital twin of a blast furnace and requires continuous synchronization of physical parameters (pressure, temperature) with the 3D model hosted in the cloud to simulate thermal efficiency in real time. The service processing chain consists of sensorIngestVF located at the edge, dataFilteringVF located at the edge, thermalSimVF located in the cloud, and model3dVF located in the cloud. Regarding operational requirements, data compression must be configured after sensorIngestVF, and payload decoding is required before thermalSimVF. For performance constraints, a latency lower than 35 ms is strictly required between sensorIngestVF and thermalSimVF. The total chain hardware consumption should ideally remain lower than 80 GB.
