The agvFleetOp provider intends to deploy the agvLogisticsBackend service to manage a fleet of 500 users. The system must manage robotic vehicles that navigate the warehouse shelves, communicating with each other and with the central server to continuously recalculate routes and optimize material picking. The processing pipeline consists of an agvTelemetryCollectorVF located in the edge, followed by a routeOptimizerVF located in the cloud, a taskDispatcherVF located in the cloud, and terminating with a centralFleetSyncVF in the cloud. Regarding service modifications, compression is strictly required after the agvTelemetryCollectorVF, security must be inserted before routeOptimizerVF, and logging is ideally applied before the centralFleetSyncVF. For operational performance, a latency of less than 30 ms is strictly required between the agvTelemetryCollectorVF and routeOptimizerVF, and routeOptimizerVF must run on a dedicated host. Finally, it is desired to maintain a bandwidth higher than 50 megabps between the agvTelemetryCollectorVF and the taskDispatcherVF.
