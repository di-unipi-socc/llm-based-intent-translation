The agvFleetOp provider intends to deploy the agvLogisticsBackend service to manage a fleet of 500 users. The system must manage robotic vehicles that navigate the warehouse shelves, communicating with each other and with the central server to continuously recalculate routes and optimize material picking. The chain begins with an agvTelemetryCollectorVF allocated at the edge, which mandates compression immediately after it. Traffic then flows to a routeOptimizerVF located in the cloud, which strictly requires security before it and must be isolated on a dedicated node. A maximum latency of 30 ms is mandatory between the agvTelemetryCollectorVF and this routeOptimizerVF. Next, the flow continues to a taskDispatcherVF located in the cloud, where a bandwidth above 50 megabps is preferred between the agvTelemetryCollectorVF and the taskDispatcherVF. The chain finishes at centralFleetSyncVF located in the cloud, which ideally requires logging before it.
