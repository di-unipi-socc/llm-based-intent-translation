The software provider devToolsOp requires a code efficiency analyzer service to manage 20,000 daily evaluation requests. This platform receives code snippets, 
executes a simulated runtime execution, and suggests specific optimizations to reduce computational resource consumption. Specifying that data caching is applied 
after the second function, the service begins with a codeReceiverVF allocated at the edge linked to a syntaxValidatorVF located in the cloud. The pipeline proceeds 
through a sandboxSimulatorVF located in the cloud and an optimizationEngineVF located in the cloud. Finally, preferring that the total chain hardware remains lower 
than 110 GB, and ensuring that data compression is applied after the final layer, the system completes the pipeline with a developersReportGeneratorVF located in 
the cloud.
