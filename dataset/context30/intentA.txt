The software provider devToolsOp requires a code efficiency analyzer service to manage 20,000 daily evaluation requests. This platform receives code snippets, 
executes a simulated runtime execution, and suggests specific optimizations to reduce computational resource consumption. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: a codeReceiverVF allocated at the edge, a syntaxValidatorVF located in the cloud, 
a sandboxSimulatorVF located in the cloud, an optimizationEngineVF located in the cloud, and a developersReportGeneratorVF located in the cloud. 
Regarding operational requirements, caching is applied after the syntaxValidatorVF to avoid processing identical code inputs. Furthermore, 
data compression is applied after the final developersReportGeneratorVF to streamline delivery. Finally, the provider prefers that the total chain hardware 
remains lower than 110 GB to minimize overall infrastructure overhead.
